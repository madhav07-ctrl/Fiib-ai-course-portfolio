# WIP 2 – Windigitaly Operational Analysis and Success Measurement

## 1. Project Overview

WIP 2 focuses on understanding Windigitaly's existing client enquiry and acquisition process. The objective is to identify operational challenges, map the journey from the first enquiry to client conversion, and define measurable success indicators for the proposed GenAI Lead Conversion Assistant.

The analysis is based on information shared by the founder of Windigitaly through email dated 15 November 2026. Figures are approximate and are identified as founder-reported information.

## 2. Existing Business Process

Windigitaly receives service enquiries through multiple sources, including:

- Meta Ads
- LinkedIn
- Referrals
- Business networking
- Instagram
- Direct outreach

The client acquisition journey consists of five main stages:

1. **Service Enquiry:** A prospective client contacts Windigitaly regarding a service.
2. **Lead Capture:** The enquiry source, contact details, and service requirements are recorded.
3. **Qualification:** The prospect is evaluated based on goals, budget, timeline, and service fit.
4. **Follow-up:** The team shares proposals, communicates next steps, and follows up with prospects.
5. **Client Decision:** The prospect becomes a client, is marked as lost, or remains under follow-up.

## 3. Founder-Reported Operational Baseline

| Business Metric | Approximate Monthly Figure |
|---|---:|
| Total enquiries | 120–150 |
| Qualified leads | 50–80 |
| Proposals shared | Around 60 |
| Clients converted | 8–10 |
| Initial time spent per enquiry | Around 10 minutes |

The average client billing varies depending on the service required, project scope, and whether the engagement is project-based or on a monthly retainer.

## 4. Key Operational Challenges

The founder identified the following challenges in the current enquiry management process:

- Managing enquiries received from different platforms.
- Maintaining complete and consistent lead information.
- Identifying genuine prospects and understanding their requirements.
- Managing multiple follow-ups and tracking conversations.
- Understanding why interested prospects do not convert into paying clients.
- Reducing repetitive manual work in enquiry handling.

Common reasons for non-conversion include budget constraints, pricing expectations, delayed decision-making, comparison with competitors, and lack of immediate requirements.

## 5. Baseline Analysis and Calculations

### A. Lead Qualification Rate

**Formula:**

Qualification Rate = Qualified Leads ÷ Total Enquiries × 100

Using the reported figures:

- Lower estimate = 50 ÷ 120 × 100 = 41.7%
- Upper estimate = 80 ÷ 150 × 100 = 53.3%

**Approximate qualification rate: 42%–53%.**

### B. Proposal-to-Client Conversion Rate

**Formula:**

Conversion Rate = Clients Converted ÷ Proposals Shared × 100

- Lower estimate = 8 ÷ 60 × 100 = 13.3%
- Upper estimate = 10 ÷ 60 × 100 = 16.7%

**Approximate proposal-to-client conversion rate: 13.3%–16.7%.**

### C. Initial Enquiry Handling Effort

**Formula:**

Monthly Handling Effort = Monthly Enquiries × Time per Enquiry

- 120 enquiries × 10 minutes = 1,200 minutes
- 150 enquiries × 10 minutes = 1,500 minutes

This equals approximately **20–25 hours per month** for initial enquiry discussions and qualification.

These calculations are estimates based on the founder-reported figures, not audited operational records.

## 6. Proposed 30-Day Pilot Targets

The following targets are proposed for evaluating the usefulness of the GenAI Lead Conversion Assistant.

| Success Measure | Proposed Target |
|---|---|
| Initial response time | Within 2 hours for 90% of enquiries |
| Lead qualification quality | At least 85% agreement with human-reviewed decisions |
| Follow-up tracking | At least 90% of leads tracked |
| Initial handling effort | 30% reduction |
| Proposal-to-client conversion | 10% relative improvement |

### Expected Impact Calculations

**A. Reduction in Initial Handling Effort**

- Existing effort: 20–25 hours per month
- Proposed reduction: 30%
- Expected time saved: 6–7.5 hours per month

**B. Improvement in Conversion Rate**

Existing conversion rate: 13.3%–16.7%

Proposed relative improvement: 10%

- 13.3% × 1.10 = approximately 14.7%
- 16.7% × 1.10 = approximately 18.3%

The proposed target range is approximately **14.7%–18.3%**.

These are pilot targets, not achieved results.

## 7. Expected Business Impact

The proposed assistant is intended to support Windigitaly in:

- Improving the visibility of incoming enquiries.
- Supporting consistent lead qualification.
- Maintaining timely follow-ups.
- Reducing repetitive initial handling work.
- Improving the tracking of lead outcomes.
- Providing better visibility into conversion performance.

The actual impact will need to be evaluated after the pilot by comparing the results against the baseline.

## 8. Conclusion

WIP 2 establishes the operational context and measurable baseline for the Windigitaly GenAI Lead Conversion Assistant. The analysis identifies gaps in lead management, qualification, follow-up, and conversion visibility.

The proposed 30-day pilot targets provide a practical framework for evaluating whether the assistant improves the enquiry management process and supports better business outcomes.
